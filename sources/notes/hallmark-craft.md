## 3. Candidate visual rules for touchstone

Provisional ids V01 to V30. Each: rule, pass condition, check, hallmark locators. Numbers are hallmark's unless marked. "script" means measurable by `ux-check.js` from the DOM, computed styles or stylesheets; several need the page emulated at another width or media setting, which `run.py` can do.

1. **V01 Running text is large enough to read.** Pass: paragraphs and body copy compute to at least 16px; no body copy under 14px; no text anywhere under 10px. Dense tables and secondary labels need a stated exception (suggest 12px floor, to be sourced). Check: script. From: typography.md#Body text rules, typography.md#Bans.
2. **V02 Lines of prose are 45 to 75 characters long.** Pass: every block of two or more lines of running text has a measured average line length in that range at 1280 wide, and at most 80. Check: script (characters per rendered line). From: typography.md#Principles, typography.md#Body text rules.
3. **V03 Line height fits the text size.** Pass: body text line-height 1.5 to 1.65; headings and display text 1.05 to 1.3; uppercase headings never below 1.0. Check: script. From: typography.md#Principles, typography.md#Body text rules.
4. **V04 A page uses at most three font families, normally two.** Pass: distinct rendered families, counting weights of one family once and monospace as one, is 3 or fewer. Check: script. From: typography.md#The 2+1 rule, typography.md#Bans.
5. **V05 A page uses at most five text sizes.** Pass: distinct computed font sizes on visible text number 5 or fewer (fluid display size counted once). Check: script. From: typography.md#Scale.
6. **V06 Headings are plainly different from body text.** Pass: each heading level differs from body text by at least one size step (ratio 1.25 or more) or by 300 or more weight units, and levels do not look alike out of order. Check: script. From: typography.md#Principles, typography.md#Weights, typography.md#Scale.
7. **V07 Body text is set plainly.** Pass: no paragraph in all capitals; no `text-align: justify` without hyphenation; body letter-spacing at most 0.05em; emphasis by bold only. Check: script. From: typography.md#Body text rules, typography.md#Weights.
8. **V08 Numbers that are compared use tabular figures.** Pass: numeric table cells and metric values compute `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` (or a monospace face). Check: script. From: typography.md#Required features. Extends DATA-007.
9. **V09 Text and images do not move the page as they load.** Pass: every `@font-face` has `font-display: swap` with a metric-matched fallback; no weight or italic is synthesised; every `img` has width and height; every declared family actually loads. Check: script. From: typography.md#Required features, typography.md#Weights, typography.md#Tone-based pairing patterns, responsive.md#Images. Extends STATE-006.
10. **V10 A large headline fits its space.** Pass: the top heading is at most 88px (96px at the limit); a heading over 50 characters is not at the largest size and over 90 is not at display size; it wraps to at most 2 lines at 414 wide and never overflows at 320. Check: script. From: typography.md#Scale, typography.md#Hero headline sizing, responsive.md#Mobile.
11. **V11 Every text and background pair passes contrast, including the faint and the flipped.** Pass: 4.5:1 for body, placeholder and helper text; 3:1 for text of 24px or 18.66px bold and up; holds inside sections with a changed background and on accent-filled buttons; every rule that sets a background also sets a text colour. Check: script. From: color.md#Contrast, interaction-and-states.md#Contrast discipline, #Token contract, #When the surface flips. Extends ACC-005.
12. **V12 Text is a solid colour with no glow.** Pass: no text with a gradient fill (`background-clip: text`) and no blurred `text-shadow` on text, so its contrast can be measured. Check: script. From: typography.md#Bans, microinteractions.md#The named tells (tell 6).
13. **V13 The focus ring is thick, contrasting and instant.** Pass: 2px or more, 3:1 against both the element and the page, offset from the element, no transition on outline or ring, never removed without replacement. Check: script. From: interaction-and-states.md#Focus rings, #State-by-state recipe, microinteractions.md#Accessibility ground truth, #The named tells (tell 17). Extends ACC-002.
14. **V14 A change of state does not change an element's size or move its neighbours.** Pass: an input's or button's bounding box is identical at rest, hovered, focused and in error; the helper or error line and any trailing icon occupy reserved space. Check: interaction (focus and invalidate each field, compare boxes). From: interaction-and-states.md#The no-layout-shift rule, #Heights and rhythm, #Labels, helper, error, #Don't, list. Extends STATE-006.
15. **V15 Hover styling applies only where hovering is possible.** Pass: `:hover` rules sit inside `@media (hover: hover)`; nothing is reachable by hover alone. Check: script (stylesheet scan). From: interaction-and-states.md#The eight states, responsive.md#Pointer and hover queries.
16. **V16 The accent colour is rare.** Pass: one accent hue (two at most) beyond neutrals and status colours; accent-filled area is about 5% or less of the viewport; the accent marks the same kinds of thing throughout. Check: script (sum the area of elements whose background is the accent, or sample a screenshot). From: color.md#Principles, #Palette construction, #Bans. Extends DATA-014. Must not stop the primary button being obvious (ACT-007).
17. **V17 A dark theme follows the system setting and stays legible.** Pass: if a dark palette exists it applies under `prefers-color-scheme: dark`; V11 passes in both modes; raised surfaces are lighter than the base. Check: script (emulate the media feature). From: responsive.md#Bans, color.md#Dark mode recipe.
18. **V18 Spacing comes from a short scale, and less space separates items within a group than separates groups.** Pass: gaps, margins and padding in use reduce to about ten values on a 4px base; within any container, inner gaps are smaller than the gap to the next group; padding is not one value at every level. Check: script. From: layout-and-space.md#Principles, #The spacing scale, #Bans. Extends DATA-004.
19. **V19 A heading lines up with what it introduces.** Pass: the heading block's left edge (or centre, for centred content) matches the content below it; no narrow centred heading above full-width left-aligned content. Check: script (compare bounding boxes). From: layout-and-space.md#Asymmetry techniques, structure.md#1. Section-heading placement. Extends DATA-004.
20. **V20 Animation is limited to transform and opacity, on named properties.** Pass: no `transition: all`; no transition or keyframe on width, height, top, left, margin, padding or border-width. Check: script. From: motion.md#Principles, #Bans, microinteractions.md#The named tells (tells 1 and 11), interaction-and-states.md#Don't, list.
21. **V21 Transitions are short.** Pass: feedback on press 80 to 150ms; hover, tooltip and menu 150 to 300ms; dialog, panel and accordion at most 500ms; an exit is no longer than its entrance; nothing over 2s that is not a loader. Check: script (computed durations). From: motion.md#Principles, #Durations, microinteractions.md#The timing canon, #Hard rules for default-on motion. Extends ACC-015.
22. **V22 Nothing moves forever or follows the pointer.** Pass: no infinite animation except a loading indicator; no parallax or scroll-scrubbed motion; no custom cursor or cursor follower; any auto-advancing content has visible controls; nothing flashes more than 3 times a second. Check: script. From: motion.md#Bans, #Scroll-linked motion, microinteractions.md#The named tells (tells 7 to 10), #Accessibility ground truth. Extends ACC-015.
23. **V23 Reduced motion removes movement.** Pass: under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` no element translates or scales, remaining fades take 150ms or less, loaders still run, and all content is visible without animation. Check: script (emulate the media feature). From: motion.md#Reduced motion, microinteractions.md#Principles, #Accessibility ground truth. Extends ACC-015.
24. **V24 A page animates in at most once, and content never waits for a scroll.** Pass: at most one entrance sequence, finished within about 500ms; no element is at `opacity: 0` until scrolled into view; at most three kinds of animation on the page; none tied to scroll below 40rem wide. Check: script for the counts and timings, judgement for whether each animation says anything. From: motion.md#Page-load orchestration, microinteractions.md#Hard rules for default-on motion, #Page-load reveals, #The named tells (tell 12), #When in doubt, cut. Extends ACC-015.
25. **V25 Keyboard feedback is immediate, and a tooltip waits only for the mouse.** Pass: focus styles, keyboard selection and error text appear with no transition; a tooltip shows at once on focus and after roughly 800 to 1000ms on hover, can be hovered, and closes on Escape. Check: interaction. From: microinteractions.md#The timing canon, #Tooltip, #The named tells (tell 16).
26. **V26 A loading indicator does not flash.** Pass: with a response forced to under 150ms no spinner appears, or any spinner shown stays at least 300ms. Check: interaction. From: microinteractions.md#The named tells (tell 15). Extends STATE-004.
27. **V27 A control's label stays on one line.** Pass: at 320, 375, 768 and 1280 wide every button, tab, nav link and breadcrumb renders on a single line without being clipped; fixed by shorter labels or a collapsed nav, not by cutting text off. Check: script (line count per control). From: responsive.md#Clickable text, #Mobile. Needs STATE-007's long-content case as its exception.
28. **V28 Touch targets are 44px.** Pass: on a coarse pointer every control's hit area is at least 44 by 44 CSS px (hallmark asks 48px height for buttons), enlarged by padding where the drawn size is smaller, including slider thumbs. Check: script. From: interaction-and-states.md#Hit targets, #Specific control overrides, microinteractions.md#Accessibility ground truth, responsive.md#Pointer and hover queries. Extends RESP-004.
29. **V29 The layout survives longer text.** Pass: with every label and heading lengthened by 35% nothing overflows, overlaps or is cut off; long words in headings break; numbers stay with their units. Check: script (lengthen text nodes, re-run the overflow check). From: responsive.md#Internationalisation, #Mobile, copy.md#Proper typography. Extends STATE-007.
30. **V30 Overlays sit where they can be used.** Pass: a dialog is centred in the viewport and no taller than 80% of it; a menu or popover is fully inside the viewport, flips near an edge, and is not clipped by an ancestor; full-height regions use `dvh`; nothing is `100vw` wide. Check: script (bounding boxes with each overlay open). From: interaction-and-states.md#Modals and overlays, #Dropdowns, tooltips, popovers, microinteractions.md#Dropdown / menu, responsive.md#Viewport units.

## 4. Taste and anti-sameness worth keeping

Each of these has no proven effect on use, but does work that matters to the purpose.

- **No headline of 90 or more characters at display size; self-written headlines at most 7 words and 50 characters** (typography.md#Hero headline sizing). A wall of giant text delays the point of the page. Script: yes.
- **No gradient text, no purple to cyan, purple to blue or orange to pink gradients** (typography.md#Bans, color.md#Bans). The most recognised generated look; it signals that nobody chose anything. Script: yes for gradient text; gradient hues are detectable from computed backgrounds with a hue test.
- **No row of three identical icon, heading, body cards as the default way to present features** (layout-and-space.md#Grids, #Bans, structure.md#Anti-patterns of structural sameness). The pattern forces content into three equal parts whether or not it has three equal parts, which is an information problem as much as a look. Script: yes (sibling cards with identical structure and width).
- **No full-viewport hero with one centred sentence** (layout-and-space.md#Bans, structure.md#Anti-patterns). It spends the first screen on nothing. Already DATA-002 for tools; worth keeping for presentation screens. Script: yes (first block height against word count).
- **No card inside a card; at most one shadow style; no glow** (layout-and-space.md#Bans, #Depth). Keeps containers meaning one thing and cuts clutter. Script: yes.
- **Banned marketing phrases and startup cliches** (copy.md#Microcopy bans, #Banned opening lines). These are sentences with no content; the research on objective text (Morkes & Nielsen 1997) supports cutting them. Script: yes (string match).
- **No stock names, no invented specifics; ask when the brief is empty** (copy.md#Microcopy bans, #Banned opening lines). Extends "nothing on screen is fake" to presentation screens. Script: partly.
- **No hover lift on things that are not clickable, and one hover signal per element** (microinteractions.md#The named tells, tells 2 and 4). Otherwise hover stops meaning "this is a control". Script: yes (hover transform on non-interactive elements).
- **Neutrals tinted toward one hue, and no pure black on pure white** (color.md#Principles, #Bans). Cheap, checkable, and the largest single contributor to a screen looking considered. Keep as a default, not a rule with severity. Script: yes.
- **One easing set and one duration set, as tokens** (motion.md#Easings, #Durations). Consistency of motion; the particular curves are preference. Script: yes (count of distinct durations and timing functions).
- **Inputs and adjacent buttons share a height** (interaction-and-states.md#Heights and rhythm). A visible misalignment reads as an error on a form. Script: yes.
- **Alignment and placement chosen on purpose** (layout-and-space.md#Asymmetry techniques, the coherence paragraph only). The general "prefer asymmetry" advice should go; the narrow point that a head must cohere with its body is kept as V19.
- **Font choice beyond the default stack** (typography.md#Banned defaults). Keep only as a prompt to choose deliberately on presentation screens. As a ban it is wrong for tools, where a system font is fast, familiar and legible. Script: yes, but should report, not fail.

## 5. Conflicts and tensions with touchstone

1. **Timed toasts for errors and undo.** microinteractions.md#Toast notification (4 to 6s dwell, "errors always get a toast") and interaction-and-states.md#Undo over confirm (Undo for 5 to 10s) against FB-004 (errors and messages with an action stay until dismissed) and FB-005 (message placed by scope).
2. **Decorative motion shipped by default.** microinteractions.md#When to ship motion by default (number count-up of 1.2 to 1.6s, endless marquee, scroll-triggered stagger, pulse, blinking caret) and structure.md#6. Reveal pattern (fade-up as default) against ACC-015 (brief, stoppable, content never hidden awaiting scroll) and against the purpose. Hallmark's own test in microinteractions.md#When in doubt, cut argues the touchstone side.
3. **Asymmetry and deliberate variation.** layout-and-space.md#Principles, #Asymmetry techniques, #When in doubt, and structure.md#The six axes, #Picking a fingerprint (no two pages alike, at most three shared axes) against DATA-004 (few shared edges), ACT-012 and TASK-011 (same thing looks and sits the same everywhere), and research G1 and G2.
4. **Submit disabled when the form is invalid.** interaction-and-states.md#Forms and #State-by-state recipe against FORM-013. Related: disabled shown by opacity 0.5 or 0.55 and removed from the tab order, against ACT-010.
5. **Generous scale against compact working screens.** typography.md#Body text rules (16px minimum, line height 1.5 to 1.65), layout-and-space.md#The spacing scale (steps to 144px), interaction-and-states.md#Heights and rhythm (44px controls everywhere) and #Labels, helper, error (a reserved empty helper line per field) against DATA-005 (about ten records in view, rows at most about 48px).
6. **Buttons that do not look like buttons.** structure.md#4. Button voice (unstyled link, typographic-only), color.md#Use of the accent (do not fill buttons; link underline only on hover) against ACT-006 and ACT-007.
7. **Skeletons preferred to spinners.** interaction-and-states.md#Loading and empty states and #Bans against touchstone's Not rules list and research H2. Nearest rule STATE-004.
8. **Loading thresholds.** copy.md#Loading (text from 2s, no text below) against STATE-004 (indicator by about 1s) and ACC-013 (loading is announced).
9. **Tables turned into cards on phones.** responsive.md#Tables on small screens against DATA-006, and against RESP-005 where comparison across rows is the task.
10. **Labels that may not wrap.** responsive.md#Clickable text (`white-space: nowrap`, shorten the label) against STATE-007 (real-world content) and COPY-007 (label specific enough to stand alone); also against hallmark's own 30 to 40% translation allowance.
11. **Root clipping.** layout-and-space.md#Page-edge clipping and responsive.md#Mobile (`overflow-x: clip` on html and body, for deliberately overflowing decoration) against RESP-001: the checker's sideways-scroll test passes while content may be cut off. If kept, the check must measure elements wider than the viewport, not scroll width.
12. **Controls revealed late.** microinteractions.md#Drag handle (handle appears after 1 to 2s of hover) against ACT-003.
13. **Floating labels.** microinteractions.md#Input focus + label float against FORM-002, and against hallmark's own interaction-and-states.md#Forms.
14. **Re-validation on every input after first blur.** microinteractions.md#Form validation, interaction-and-states.md#Forms against FORM-006, unless limited to clearing an existing error.
15. **Spinner replaces the button label.** interaction-and-states.md#Loading and empty states against ACT-001 and FB-001 (and hallmark's own eight-states table).
16. **Required decoration.** interaction-and-states.md#Loading and empty states (an illustration or icon in every empty state), color.md#Use of the accent (a small square beside headings), copy.md#Empty states (a line explaining what the thing is) against COPY-003, COPY-005 and TASK-012. Imagery itself is not required by these nine files; "none" is an allowed image treatment.
17. **Voice.** copy.md#Voice samples per tone (puns, salutations, refusals as style) against COPY-006 in any tool. Acceptable on a presentation screen only where the line still carries information.
18. **Headings out of position.** structure.md#1. Section-heading placement (bottom-aligned, inline with body, overlapping an image) against COPY-013, ACC-005 and layer-cake scanning (research F5).
19. **Font bans.** typography.md#Banned defaults and #Bans (no system stack, no single-font page) against touchstone's instruction that an existing design system outranks a minor rule, and against research G2. No rule id.
20. **Typographic punctuation.** typography.md#Required features and copy.md#Proper typography require long dashes and curly quotes; the owner's global instruction forbids long dashes in any output. No touchstone rule id; a touchstone rule on punctuation must follow the owner's instruction.
21. **Hallmark against itself**, to settle before adopting numbers: accent area 3% or 5%; display line height 1.05 to 1.2 or 1.1 to 1.3; display cap 5.5rem, 6rem, 7rem or 9rem; focus offset 1px, 2px or 3px; disabled opacity 0.5 or 0.55; target 44px or 48px; reduced motion 150ms or 0.01s; "nine steps" listing ten and "six levels" listing seven; infinite loops banned in motion.md and shipped in microinteractions.md; animate only transform and opacity, yet animate grid rows.

## 6. My reading

- Most valuable: the measurable typography floor (size, measure, line height, family and size counts), the contrast failure list (flipped surfaces, accent fills, placeholders), the no-layout-shift rules for control states, and the motion limits. Nearly all are script checks touchstone lacks.
- Second: the "named tells" in microinteractions.md. About half are functional and checkable from stylesheets (`transition: all`, layout-property animation, animated focus rings, spinner flash, hover on non-controls).
- The copy bans are worth taking whole as an extension of COPY-006 to presentation screens; they have the best research backing of anything in these files (Morkes & Nielsen 1997).
- Redundant with touchstone: most of interaction-and-states.md (forms, focus, targets, undo, empty states), the accessibility lists, and copy.md's principles. Touchstone's versions are stricter and sourced.
- Drop: the font catalogue and pairing tables, theme multipliers, spring settings, voice samples, the six-axis fingerprint scheme and the domain table. They serve variety between sites, not the reader of one screen.
- Drop or reverse: default-on motion, "prefer asymmetry", timed error toasts, disabled submit, skeleton preference, floating labels, tables to cards.
- Almost nothing here has research behind its numbers. The figures are practice or the author's preference, and hallmark disagrees with itself on several. Any touchstone rule built on them should say `practice-consensus` at most and pick one number.
- The one idea to carry over as a principle: hallmark's own test, "what is lost if this were instant", generalises to every decorative element and fits touchstone's purpose exactly.
