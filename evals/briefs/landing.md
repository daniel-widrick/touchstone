# Brief: product page for Ledgerline

Build the public product page for Ledgerline. It is the page people land on from a search or a recommendation, on a phone as often as on a desktop.

Everything we can tell you about the product:

- Ledgerline is invoicing software for small building and trades firms, typically 2 to 20 people.
- A tradesperson can write an invoice on their phone on site, from a quote or from scratch, and send it by email or text before they leave.
- It works without a signal and sends when the phone is back online.
- It chases late payers: polite reminders at 7, 14 and 30 days after the due date, which the firm can switch off per customer.
- It handles the Construction Industry Scheme deduction and reverse-charge VAT on the invoice, and exports to Xero, QuickBooks and Sage.
- Price: 12 GBP per user per month, billed monthly, cancel any time. The first 30 days are free and no card is needed to start.
- The company is Ledgerline Ltd, founded in Leeds in 2021. Support is by email at help@ledgerline.example, answered on working days.
- Starting a trial happens at `/signup`. Existing customers sign in at `/login`.

That is all we have. We do not have photography, a logo file, or screenshots yet.

## Constraints

- Deliver one self-contained file: HTML with inline CSS and JavaScript. No build step, no frameworks or libraries, no network requests, no external fonts or images.
- It will be read on phones as often as on desktops.
