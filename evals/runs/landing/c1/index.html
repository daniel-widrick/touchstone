Job done, invoice gone.

The longer an invoice waits for an evening at the kitchen table, the longer you wait to be paid. Ledgerline gets it out while you are still on the job.

  1. Write it on your phone

    Turn a quote into an invoice, or start from scratch. Either way you do it on site.

  2. Send it before you leave

    By email or by text. If there is no signal, it goes as soon as your phone is back online.

  3. Let it do the chasing

    Polite reminders go out 7, 14 and 30 days after the due date, so you do not have to make the awkward call.

Common questions

Do I need a card to start the trial?

No. The first 30 days are free and you do not need a card to start.

What does it cost after the trial?

£12 per user per month, billed monthly. There is no contract and you can cancel any time.

What happens if I have no signal on site?

Carry on as normal. Ledgerline works without a signal and sends the invoice when your phone is back online.

Can I stop reminders going to a particular customer?

Yes. Reminders go out 7, 14 and 30 days after the due date, and you can switch them off customer by customer.

Does it work with my accounts software?

Ledgerline exports to Xero, QuickBooks and Sage.

Does it deal with CIS and reverse-charge VAT?

Yes. Both the Construction Industry Scheme deduction and reverse-charge VAT are handled on the invoice.
