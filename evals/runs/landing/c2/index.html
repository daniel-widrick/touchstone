Questions

Before you try it

Something else? Email help@ledgerline.example and we'll answer on working days.

Do I need a card to start the trial?

No. The first 30 days are free and you don't need a card to start.

What does it cost after the 30 days?

£12 per user per month, billed monthly. There's no contract and you can cancel any time.

What happens if there's no signal on site?

Ledgerline works without a signal. Write and send the invoice as usual, and it goes out when your phone is back online.

Can I stop reminders going to a particular customer?

Yes. Reminders go out at 7, 14 and 30 days after the due date, and you can switch them off customer by customer.

Does it deal with CIS and reverse-charge VAT?

Yes. The Construction Industry Scheme deduction and reverse-charge VAT are both handled on the invoice.

Which accounting software does it work with?

Ledgerline exports to Xero, QuickBooks and Sage.

How do I get help?
