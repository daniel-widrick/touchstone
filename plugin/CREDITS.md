# Credits

Touchstone's rules are written in our own words. Each rule cites the source it
rests on, with a locator, in its `Sources` line. The sources include:

- US Department of Health and Human Services and GSA, *Research-Based Web
  Design and Usability Guidelines*, 2006. US government work.
- Smith and Mosier, *Guidelines for Designing User Interface Software*, MITRE
  for the US Air Force, 1986. Approved for public release.
- W3C, *Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2*, the ACT Rules and the
  WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices. W3C document licence.
- Microsoft, *Windows User Experience Interaction Guidelines*. Restated from
  the MicrosoftDocs/win32 repository, licensed CC BY 4.0.
- Bargas-Avila et al., *Simple but Crucial User Interfaces in the World Wide
  Web: Introducing 20 Guidelines for Usable Web Form Design*, InTech, 2010,
  CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, and Seckler et al., CHI 2014.
- Published design-system guidance from GOV.UK, USWDS, IBM Carbon, GitHub
  Primer, Atlassian, Apple, Microsoft Fluent, Shopify and Vercel; articles by
  Nielsen Norman Group and Baymard Institute; and the papers listed in the
  project's `sources/registry.yaml`.
- *hallmark*, an earlier design skill by the same author, MIT licence.

Where a rule rests on a judgement made while reviewing real results rather
than on a source, it cites `house:` and is marked as a house preference.
